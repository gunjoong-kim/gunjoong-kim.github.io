I am Gunjoong Kim, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at Yonsei University. I am supervised by Prof. Hojung Cha at the [Mobile Embedded Systems Lab](https://mobed.yonsei.ac.kr/index.php?mid=Homepage). I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Yonsei University in February 2026 and my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Seoul in February 2024.

## Research Interests

I build mobile systems that bring context-aware intelligence and immersive experiences into everyday use, adapting to each user’s needs. My research spans on-device AI systems and immersive computing. Across these areas, I explore how models and hardware shape system design, and how these insights can inform the abstractions and principles for building adaptive, personalized mobile systems.
